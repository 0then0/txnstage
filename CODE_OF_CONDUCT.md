# Code of Conduct

TxnStage welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. This code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other community spaces maintained by the project, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Treat participants respectfully and discuss technical disagreements with evidence.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and respect other people's time and boundaries.
- Make room for newcomers, correct mistakes candidly and accept reasonable corrections.
- Respect privacy. Share another person's personal information only with their permission.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexualized attention, impersonation, doxxing and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Criticism of an idea or implementation is welcome; attacks on a person are not.

## Reporting a concern

Contact a repository maintainer privately using a contact method they publish on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/0then0). If no private method is available, open a minimal issue asking for a confidential contact channel, without naming affected people or describing private details. Security vulnerabilities belong in the separate [security reporting channel](SECURITY.md).

Include relevant links or evidence privately where possible. Maintainers will limit sharing of reports to people needed to address the concern, subject to safety and legal obligations. Do not retaliate against someone for reporting a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, ask participants to stop, restrict participation or block accounts. They will consider the behavior, its impact, repetition and the safety of participants. Serious threats or harassment can result in immediate restriction. A participant may privately ask a maintainer to reconsider a decision; maintainers should avoid handling reports about their own conduct where another maintainer is available.
